Welcome

The purpose of this website is not to persuade.

The purpose of this website is to preserve information which has become increasingly difficult to locate through ordinary means.

Many visitors arrive here already familiar with the official account of Vincent Whittman's life and career. Such visitors are encouraged to continue examining the historical record carefully and without prejudice.

For more than half a century, newspapers, broadcasters, documentary filmmakers and academic historians have repeated the same narrative concerning Mr. Whittman and the events surrounding the final years of his career.

We simply ask whether this narrative is complete.

"History is not written by those who witnessed the event. History is written by those who remain."

The surviving television archives present many unanswered questions.

Why do contemporary witness accounts differ so dramatically from later reports?

Why were large portions of the original broadcast material removed from public circulation?

Why did so many individuals who worked directly with Mr. Whittman describe him in terms that differ substantially from the image now presented in popular media?

The reader must decide for himself.

The Question of Vision

Students of early broadcasting frequently remarked upon Mr. Whittman's unusual fascination with the future of entertainment.

Years before such technologies existed, he spoke publicly about personalized broadcasting, twenty-four hour programming, interactive audiences and entertainment systems present within every home.

Most historians regard these statements as educated speculation.

Others remain unconvinced.

Visitors interested in this subject may wish to review archived interviews and production notes contained within the Records section.
